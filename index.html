Fair questions

QIs it really free?+
Yes — 100% free. While we grow toward 1,000 members, every webinar, discussion and bit of guidance is on the house. The goal right now is a thriving community, not revenue.
QWho is this for?+
Anyone in or aspiring to be in data — analysts, data engineers, data scientists, ML/AI and GenAI builders, and career-switchers or freshers finding their footing. If you're curious about data, you belong.
QWhy connect on LinkedIn to join?+
We vet members to keep the group high-signal and spam-free. A quick look at your LinkedIn profile is the lightest way to do that. A self-serve join form is coming soon.
QWhat's the catch? Selling courses?+
No catch, no course-selling, no MLM. The community is tool-neutral and unsponsored in spirit — MLDeep Systems simply powers it so the mentorship stays free.
QHow much time does it take?+
As little or as much as you like. Drop in for a webinar, lurk on discussions, or jump into everything — zero pressure, no attendance requirements.
