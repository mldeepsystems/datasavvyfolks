Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Is it really free?
Yes — 100% free. While we grow toward 1,000 members, every webinar, discussion and bit of guidance is on the house. Our goal right now is a thriving community, not revenue.
Who is this community for?
Anyone in or aspiring to be in data — analysts, data engineers, data scientists, ML/AI and GenAI builders, and career-switchers or freshers finding their footing. If you're curious about data, you belong here.
Why do I have to connect on LinkedIn to join?
We vet members to keep the group high-signal and spam-free. A quick look at your LinkedIn profile is the lightest way to do that. (A self-serve join form is coming soon.)
What's the catch? Is anyone selling courses?
No catch, no course-selling, no MLM. The community is tool-neutral and unsponsored in spirit — MLDeep Systems simply powers it so we can keep the mentorship free.
How much time does it take?
As little or as much as you like. Drop in for a webinar, lurk on discussions, or jump into every conversation — there's zero pressure and no attendance requirements.
